## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out Harbourline's commitment to protecting the information it holds about its customers, staff, suppliers and operations. It is the top-level policy of the information security management system (ISMS), and every other security policy sits under it.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Harbourline staff, contractors and third parties who access Harbourline information or systems, in every location, including remote work.

## 3. Policy statements

3.1 Harbourline will protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of its information in proportion to the risk.

3.2 Harbourline will run an ISMS aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and will keep a Statement of Applicability showing which Annex A controls apply and why.

3.3 Information security risks will be identified, assessed and treated using the enterprise risk management framework. Each risk will have a named owner and will be rated against the Board's risk appetite.

3.4 Harbourline will meet its legal, regulatory and contractual obligations, including the Privacy Act 1988 and the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

3.5 Information security objectives will be set each year, measured and reported to the Board.

3.6 All staff will complete security awareness training at induction and every year after that.

3.7 Suspected security incidents must be reported straight away, in line with POL-05 Incident Management Policy.

3.8 Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board of Directors | Sets risk appetite, approves this policy and receives ISMS reporting |
| Chief Executive Officer | Owns this policy and ensures the ISMS has the resources it needs |
| Head of IT Infrastructure | Runs the ISMS day to day and reports on security objectives |
| Privacy Officer | Oversees privacy obligations and data breach assessments |
| Managers | Make sure their teams understand and follow security policies |
| All staff and contractors | Follow security policies and report incidents and weaknesses |

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 5.2 and Annex A controls A.5.1, A.5.2 and A.5.4. Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed every year by the owner, and after any significant incident, audit finding or business change.
