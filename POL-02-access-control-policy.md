## 1. Purpose

This policy makes sure that only the right people have access to Harbourline's systems and information, and only for as long as they need it.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers all Harbourline systems, including cloud services, the Microsoft 365 tenant, the customer portal, remote access and all non-production environments.

## 3. Policy statements

### Access principles
3.1 Access is granted on least privilege and need to know.

3.2 Every user has a unique account. Shared accounts are not allowed unless an exception is approved and recorded in the risk register.

### Joining, moving and leaving
3.3 Access is requested through the service desk and approved by the user's manager and the system owner.

3.4 When someone changes role, access no longer needed is removed within five business days.

3.5 When someone leaves, all access is removed on their last day. Contractor access expires automatically on the contract end date.

### Authentication
3.6 Multi-factor authentication is required for all users of Microsoft 365, remote access, the customer portal and any internet-facing system. Phishing-resistant methods are required for privileged accounts.

3.7 Legacy authentication protocols that cannot enforce multi-factor authentication are blocked.

3.8 Passwords and passphrases must meet the current standard published by IT and must never be shared or reused across systems.

### Privileged access
3.9 Administrative accounts are separate from everyday user accounts and are not used for email or web browsing.

3.10 Privileged access is granted for a defined period and reviewed every quarter.

### Non-production environments
3.11 Development, test and staging environments must not be reachable from the internet unless access is restricted, for example through the VPN or an IP allowlist.

3.12 Non-production environments must not hold real personal information unless the Privacy Officer approves it.

### Review
3.13 System owners review user access every six months and privileged access every quarter. The results are kept as evidence.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Head of IT Infrastructure | Owns this policy and the identity platform |
| System owners | Approve access and complete access reviews |
| Managers | Request and approve access for their teams, and tell IT about role changes and departures |
| People and Culture | Tells IT about joiners, movers and leavers on time |
