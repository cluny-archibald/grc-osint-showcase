## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what staff can and cannot do with Harbourline's information, devices and systems.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all staff and contractors using Harbourline devices, accounts or information, whether in the office, on the road or working remotely.

## 3. Policy statements

### General use
3.1 Harbourline systems are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it doesn't interfere with work, break the law or put Harbourline at risk.

3.2 Staff must not try to get around security controls such as antivirus, multi-factor authentication or web filtering.

### Devices and remote work
3.3 Laptops and phones that access Harbourline information must be enrolled in device management, encrypted and kept up to date.

3.4 Devices must be locked when left unattended. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT straight away.

3.5 When working remotely, staff must use the VPN or approved cloud services and must take care that others can't see or overhear confidential information.

### Information handling
3.6 Customer and staff personal information must only be stored in approved systems.

3.7 Harbourline information must not be sent to personal email accounts or stored on personal cloud services.

### Code and public platforms
3.8 Source code, configuration files and internal system details must not be published on public platforms such as GitHub, GitLab or package registries unless the Head of Digital approves it.

3.9 Credentials, keys and internal hostnames must never be committed to any code repository, public or private.

### AI tools
3.10 Only AI tools approved by IT may be used with Harbourline information. Confidential or personal information must not be entered into unapproved AI tools.

3.11 Output from AI tools must be checked by a person before it is used in decisions, sent to customers or published.

### Training
3.12 All staff complete security awareness training at induction and every year after that, and must complete any extra training required for their role.

## 4. Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## 5. Acknowledgement

All staff must read this policy and confirm they accept it at induction and after each major revision.

## 6. References

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls A.5.10 Acceptable use of information and other associated assets, A.6.3 Information security awareness, education and training, A.6.7 Remote working, A.8.1 User endpoint devices and A.8.12 Data leakage prevention.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed every year by the owner, with input from the Head of IT Infrastructure.
