## 1. Purpose

This policy makes sure suppliers who access, process or host Harbourline information are assessed before they're engaged, and managed throughout the relationship.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers all suppliers that access Harbourline systems or information. That includes cloud and software services, managed service providers, contractors, and third-party scripts and services embedded in Harbourline's websites and apps.

## 3. Policy statements

### Supplier register
3.1 Procurement keeps a register of every supplier in scope, with the service provided, the information involved, the business owner and a risk tier.

### Risk tiers
3.2 Each supplier is given a tier based on the information it handles and how critical its service is:

| Tier | Typical supplier | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Holds personal or confidential information, or runs a critical service | Full due diligence before engagement, reviewed every year |
| 2 | Limited access to internal information | Questionnaire before engagement, reviewed every two years |
| 3 | No access to Harbourline information | Basic checks at onboarding |

### Due diligence
3.3 Due diligence for Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers covers:
- ownership and jurisdiction
- where data is stored and processed (data residency)
- sub-processors and how the supplier manages them
- security certifications or independent assurance reports
- breach history and incident notification arrangements

3.4 Where personal information will be disclosed overseas, the Privacy Officer must confirm the arrangement meets APP 8 before the supplier is engaged.

### Contracts
3.5 Contracts with Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers must include security requirements, breach notification timeframes, the right to audit or receive assurance, and data return or deletion at the end of the contract.

### Website and app components
3.6 New third-party scripts or services added to Harbourline websites or apps must go through this policy first. Digital reviews embedded third-party components every six months and removes any that are no longer needed.

### Ongoing management
3.7 Business owners monitor supplier performance and tell Procurement about significant changes, such as a change of ownership, a new sub-processor or a security incident.

3.8 When a supplier relationship ends, access is removed and the return or deletion of Harbourline information is confirmed in writing.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Procurement Manager | Owns this policy and the supplier register |
| Business owners | Request suppliers, provide information for due diligence and manage the relationship |
| Head of IT Infrastructure | Assesses security responses and technical controls |
| Privacy Officer | Reviews arrangements involving personal information, including overseas disclosure |
