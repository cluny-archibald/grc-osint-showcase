## 1. Purpose

This policy makes sure that information security incidents are reported, assessed and handled quickly and consistently, and that Harbourline meets its notification obligations.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers any event that affects, or could affect, the confidentiality, integrity or availability of Harbourline information or systems. That includes incidents at suppliers that involve Harbourline information.

## 3. Policy statements

### Reporting
3.1 Anyone who suspects a security incident must report it to the IT service desk straight away, by phone or through the service desk portal. Staff must not investigate on their own or try to fix the issue themselves.

3.2 Suppliers must report incidents involving Harbourline information within the timeframe set in their contract.

### Assessment
3.3 The Head of IT Infrastructure, or a delegate, assesses each report and assigns a severity:

| Severity | Example |
|---|---|
| High | Confirmed compromise, ransomware, or personal information exposed |
| Medium | Contained malware, a successful phishing attempt with no data accessed |
| Low | A blocked attack or a policy breach with no impact |

3.4 High severity incidents are escalated to the Chief Executive Officer and the Privacy Officer straight away, and the incident response team is activated.

### Response
3.5 Incidents are handled through these stages: contain, eradicate, recover and review. Actions and decisions are logged with times.

3.6 Evidence is collected and kept in a way that preserves its integrity, in case it's needed for legal, insurance or regulatory purposes.

### Notification
3.7 Where personal information may be involved, the Privacy Officer assesses whether it's an eligible data breach under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. The assessment is completed within 30 days. If it is eligible, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and affected individuals are notified as soon as practicable.

3.8 The Chief Executive Officer decides on notification to customers, insurers, the Board and law enforcement, with advice from the incident response team.

3.9 If Harbourline is a responsible entity for a critical infrastructure asset under the Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018, it will report cyber security incidents to ASD within the timeframes the Act requires.

### Learning
3.10 Every High and Medium severity incident gets a post-incident review within ten business days. Lessons learned are recorded, and actions are added to the risk register with owners and due dates.

### Testing
3.11 The incident response plan is tested at least once a year.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Head of IT Infrastructure | Owns this policy and leads the incident response team |
| Privacy Officer | Assesses data breaches and manages notifications under the Privacy Act |
| Chief Executive Officer | Makes major decisions and approves external notifications |
| Communications Manager | Manages messages to customers, staff and the media |
| All staff and contractors | Report suspected incidents straight away |
